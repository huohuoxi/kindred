# Two-minute demo guide

All actions are simulations. Choose a fictional identity in the header. Changing identity changes the viewer, not the dataset. Wallets start with $1,000 before seeded activity; balances already reflect committed and settled seeded gifts.

## Create → share → join → settle

1. On Discover, choose **Try a demo story**. Its checklist links use the normal screens and commands.
2. Switch to Maya and open Create with the first-N template. The four steps retain input when using Back and Continue. Confirm the precise condition, UTC event deadline, $10 per person, three slots, and evidence requirements. Review the $30 debit and check the simulation notice before committing.
3. The new wish opens with a share dialog. **Copy promise** copies its complete readable text and demo homepage. This wish is browser-local; sending its custom URL does not create synchronized participation. Escape closes the dialog and returns focus.
4. Switch to Alex and choose **Join this wish · free**. Joining reserves one slot with no wallet debit. Alex cannot join twice; Maya cannot join her own wish.
5. Choose **Submit evidence · propose YES** and **Fill sample evidence**. The sample is fictional. Its occurrence time is no later than the event deadline or demo clock. Submit to close enrollment and open the 24-hour objection window.
6. In the separate **Demo controls** panel, choose **Past review window**. Confirm the displayed new time; it affects all wishes. Choose **Finalize YES · send gifts** as simulated human reviewer.
7. Inspect the receipt: with only Alex enrolled, $10 gifted, $20 returned to Maya, $30 reconciled, and $0 escrow. **View wallet activity** shows the transaction. Switch Maya/Alex to see both sides.

## Failure and full refund

Open **Your first finish line is just the start** from Discover (stable ID `your-first-finish-line`). In Demo controls, advance **Past evidence grace period**, confirming the global time. Submit **Propose NO · refund**. Advance **Past review window**, confirm, and finalize NO. The receipt returns all $75 to Jordan, with $0 gifted and $0 escrow. You can also follow the same path with a newly created wish.

The deadline is the last valid event-occurrence time; the 72-hour grace period permits late reporting only. NO is unavailable until strictly after grace. Advancing the clock never transfers money automatically.

## Objections and arbitration

The **One more encore for the neighborhood** seed starts disputed. Its objection blocks normal payout. Read the evidence and Jordan’s objection, then use **Demo controls → Simulated human arbitrator**.

- **Uphold** with a rationale settles the proposed outcome.
- **Reject** with a rationale archives the proposal and keeps all funds held. Enrollment remains closed. If grace has ended, only a new NO proposal is available; it then needs review.

To create your own objection, submit evidence on a new wish and switch to its creator or eligible recipient before the review deadline. Choose **Object with a reason**. A person cannot object twice on the same proposal.

## Other gift modes

- **Community template:** creates an equal-split pool. Per-person figures remain estimates during enrollment. Remaining cents go in stable enrollment order. Very small pools may produce zero-cent shares.
- **Milestone template:** creates a personal gift to one other fictional person. No public join action is shown; the designated person is eligible automatically.

## Clock, persistence, and reset

The clock is simulated and advances only through Demo controls. It is shared by all wishes in this browser and cannot move backward. Review, grace, and event-deadline controls display the resulting time before applying it.

Reload restores ordinary progress. **Demo controls → Reset demo** asks for confirmation because it deletes all browser-local creations and restores coherent seed balances, wishes, and dates. Cancel preserves progress. If saved data is corrupt or incompatible, the recovery banner directs you to this reset without silently overwriting it.

Browser storage may be unavailable or full; the visible save-error banner means changes are in memory only. Multiple concurrent tabs editing the demo are unsupported.
